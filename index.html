Naija Shopping Festival 2026 – The Ultimate Christmas Shopping Experience

Welcome to the Konga Naija Shopping Festival 2026, Nigeria's biggest end-of-year shopping event and your one-stop destination for the best Christmas deals, holiday hampers and festive bundles. As the year comes to a close, Konga is bringing you unbeatable discounts across every category, from 7 December right through the Christmas season, so you can celebrate in style without stretching your budget.

Whether you are looking for the perfect gift, upgrading your home appliances before the in-laws arrive, getting your owambe outfit ready for Detty December, or stocking up on food and drinks for the festive season, the Naija Shopping Festival has massive savings on top-quality, authentic products.

🎄 Everything You Need for the Festive Season

• Christmas Hampers & Festive Bundles – Ready-made hampers for family, friends, staff and clients, plus value bundles on food and drinks.
• Gifts for Everyone – Shop by recipient or budget, from stocking fillers under ₦10,000 to premium gifts.
• Phones & Gadgets – The latest smartphones, tablets, laptops, earbuds and smartwatches.
• Home & Kitchen Appliances – Refrigerators, freezers, cookers, blenders, TVs, generators and inverters.
• Festive Fashion – Owambe-ready outfits, shoes, bags, watches and accessories.
• Food, Drinks & Groceries – Rice, oil, beverages, wines and everything for your Christmas table.
• Kids & Toys – Toys, games, bikes and Christmas clothes for the little ones.

🎁 Why Shop the Naija Shopping Festival on Konga?

• Massive Price Slashes – Festival-only discounts and daily flash sales across every category.
• Free Delivery Deals – Free delivery on selected items, delivered nationwide to your doorstep.
• Bulk & Corporate Orders – Special pricing on bulk purchases and corporate hampers for your team and clients.
• Top Trusted Brands – Shop authentic products from official brand stores and verified sellers.
• Flexible, Secure Payment – Pay with KongaPay, Konga Wallet, Verve, Mastercard, Visa or on delivery.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Naija Shopping Festival 2026?

The Naija Shopping Festival 2026 kicks off on 7 December 2026 and runs through the Christmas season. New deals drop daily, so check back often.

Do I get free delivery?

Selected festival items come with free delivery. Look for the free delivery tag on the product page. Delivery fees on other items are shown at checkout.

Can I buy hampers or gifts in bulk for my staff or clients?

Yes. For corporate hampers and bulk orders, use our Bulk Purchase service and our team will help with pricing, packaging and delivery.

How can I pay?

Pay securely with KongaPay, your Konga Wallet, Verve, Mastercard or Visa cards, or pay on delivery where available.

What if I need to return an item?

Festival purchases are covered by the Konga Return Policy. Visit the Return Policy page for eligibility and timelines.
